Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use DrumDash, we may collect information you provide directly, such as your email address when you contact us. We also collect certain technical information automatically, including your device type, operating system, browser type, and usage data to help improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve DrumDash, to respond to your comments and questions, and to send technical notices and support messages. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Your song imports, scores, and app settings are stored locally on your device. If you use the Android app with cloud sync enabled, your progress data is stored securely on our servers. We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide the service or comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third-Party Services

DrumDash uses Google Play Services for Android distribution and Unity Analytics for anonymous usage statistics. These services have their own privacy policies governing data collection. We do not control how these third parties process your data. We encourage you to review their privacy policies for more information.

5. Your Rights (CCPA / GDPR)

If you are a California resident (CCPA) or a resident of the European Economic Area (GDPR), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Know: You may request information about the personal data we collect, use, and share.
  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal data, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Opt-Out: We do not sell your personal information. However, you may opt out of analytics tracking at any time via our Privacy Settings page.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request your data in a commonly used format.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We will respond to your request within 30 days as required by applicable law.

6. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for essential site functionality. We also use analytics cookies (Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use the site. You can manage your cookie preferences on our Privacy Settings page. For more details, please see our Cookie Policy.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

DrumDash is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us at support@northshorepress.co.
