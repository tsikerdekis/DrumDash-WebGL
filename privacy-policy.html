Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2024

1. Information We Collect

When you use DrumDash, we may collect information you provide directly, such as your email address when you contact us. We also collect certain technical information automatically, including your device type, operating system, browser type, and usage data to help improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve DrumDash, to respond to your comments and questions, and to send technical notices and support messages.

3. Data Storage

Your song imports, scores, and app settings are stored locally on your device. If you use the Android app with cloud sync enabled, your progress data is stored securely on our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

DrumDash uses Google Play Services for Android distribution and Unity Analytics for anonymous usage statistics. These services have their own privacy policies governing data collection.

5. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for essential site functionality. For more details, please see our Cookie Policy.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us.
