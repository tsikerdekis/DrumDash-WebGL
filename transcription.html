Accuracy You Can Hear

Every drum part is scored against hand-checked ground truth on songs the model has never seen. This is how the transcription engine currently performs.

🥁 Kick 91%
🥁 Snare 90%
🎩 Hi-Hat 81%
🎵 Ride 79%
💥 Crash 78%
🥁 Floor Tom 67%
🥁 Mid Tom 59%
🥁 High Tom 56%

Scores are F1 measurements on held-out songs, with a note counted as correct when it lands within ± 100 ms of the real hit. Kick and snare — the backbone of every groove — are detected most reliably, while individual toms remain the toughest part of the kit to separate.
