Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2024

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They help websites function properly and improve your browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

DrumDash uses minimal cookies for essential site functionality, such as remembering your cookie consent preference. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

3. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use analytics services (such as Cloudflare) that set their own cookies. These are used for performance monitoring and security.

5. Contact

If you have questions about our cookie usage, please contact us.
