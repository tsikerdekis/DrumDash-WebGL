Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2024

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They help websites function properly and improve your browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

DrumDash uses cookies for essential site functionality, such as remembering your cookie consent preference. We also use Google Analytics cookies to collect anonymous usage data (page views, browser type, session information) to help us understand how visitors use the site and improve the experience. We do not use cookies for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We use Google Analytics, a third-party analytics service provided by Google LLC, which sets cookies to collect anonymous usage data. Google Analytics cookies help us understand page performance, user behavior, and site traffic patterns. Google may process this data in accordance with its privacy policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics cookies at any time via our Privacy Settings page.

5. Contact

If you have questions about our cookie usage, please contact us.
