Drumming Reimagined

DrumDash lets you explore your percussion skills freely in a flexible and responsive environment. Import any song, choose your difficulty, and play the way that works best for you.

Your Music, Your Charts

Load MIDI files, MP3, WAV, or OGG directly into DrumDash. Our AI engine analyzes the track's tempo, structure, and rhythm to generate a playable drum chart in seconds. Every song you love becomes a lesson.

Four Smart Difficulty Levels

Easy, Normal, Hard, and Expert — each tailored to the song's unique structure. Beginners build fundamentals on simple patterns while advanced players tackle intricate fills and polyrhythms. The AI learns from millions of drum patterns to keep every chart musically authentic.

Any Input, Any Setup

Import from Paradiddle, Clone Hero, audio files, or MIDI. DrumDash adapts to your gear and your library — no expensive hardware required to get started. Connect via USB or Bluetooth and play with latency-free precision.

Real Feedback, Real Improvement

Structured lessons, accuracy scoring, and achievement badges help you see real improvement over time. From your first beat to complex fills, DrumDash guides your drumming journey.
